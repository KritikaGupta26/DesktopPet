# Water Panda v17

Water, movement, personal reminders and ordinary chatter now draw on the panda's own transparent canvas. They do not create separate reminder/dialogue windows. Panda Home remains an explicitly opened management window for settings and history.

Water controls: 100/200/300 ml logs intake, Not yet declines, 10 min later snoozes without logging. Personal and movement reminders retain Done and 10 min later. The water reveal plays once and holds the final glass pose. The panda and cloud move together when dragged. Expansion preserves the pet's position where screen space allows; it clamps at edges.

Integrated from the uploaded sprite collection: eight water-offer frames, eight hoop frames and six wristwatch frames. Crops preserve the whole character and use a shared scale per sequence with a grounded baseline. Explicit sources, timings and modes are in assets/reminder_animations.json. Other draft sheets in the collection have not been integrated by this update.

Validation: 17 automated checks, including absence of new reminder/dialogue windows, water snooze scheduling without history writes, cloud action bindings, and holding the final offer frame. Actual Windows desktop rendering, dragging at different DPI values and multi-monitor placement have not been executed in this Linux workspace.
