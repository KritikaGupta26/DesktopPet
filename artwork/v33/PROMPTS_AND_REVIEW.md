# v33 artwork and review

Built-in image generation created eight individual right-profile running poses, a pillar peekaboo sheet, and four wardrobe component atlases. The character reference was the existing fluffy panda. Eight run phases explicitly specify near/far opposite arm-leg motion, contact/compression/passing/flight, mirrored counterparts, complete ears/feet and stable scale. The first atlas run draft was rejected because of repeated poses.

The wardrobe atlases contain front/profile sleeveless garment or scarf components and front/profile headgear for all sixteen themes. References are the v32 seasonal costumes. Generated fabric textures and alpha cutouts are preserved. Fitting is performed in the application through wardrobe.py, with reviewed overrides for merged/occluded fur regions. Glass, bamboo, dark foreground paws and the pillar remain in front of clothes. Torso and head accessories follow each animated pose; they are not an idle-body replacement.

Peekaboo: notice pillar, approach, crouch behind it with belly/ears exposed, peek left, tuck back, peek right, step out and proud finish. One shared original pillar component is used to avoid changing its physical size. The scene has a wide canvas while panda body height remains the same.

The thematic additions include finite, silent click-through Diwali/New Year/birthday fireworks and Holi powder puffs. Reminders cancel effects, and effects have a setting. These are code-native particle animation rather than bitmap stickers.

Ideas inspired by Kung Fu Panda: clumsy confidence, balancing practice, hiding behind something too narrow, short training moves, a stumble-and-proud-recovery, and food-motivated practice. These are original desktop-panda behaviour ideas, not claims that every suggested activity has been implemented. References: https://www.dreamworks.com/movies/kung-fu-panda and https://www.dreamworks.com/movies/kung-fu-panda-3
